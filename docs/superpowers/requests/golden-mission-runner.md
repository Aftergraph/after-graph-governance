# Build request: Golden Mission runner

Owner: `Aftergraph/works-execution`
Contract: `golden-mission/0.1` (registry: `docs/golden-mission/0.1.json` in after-graph-governance)
Acceptance vectors: `GOLDEN-001`, `GOLDEN-002`, `GOLDEN-003`

## What to build

A mission runner that executes the registered Golden Mission branches
(`success`, `refusal`, `revocation`, `crash-recovery`, `verifier-failure`)
as durable execution in `Aftergraph/works-execution`, preserving canonical
identity across every consequential seam and failing closed on identity
breaks. Verification participation comes from the independent verification
owners; the runner never verifies its own output.

## Acceptance

- `GOLDEN-001`: the full success chain with preserved canonical identity is accepted.
- `GOLDEN-002`: principal drift across a consequential seam is rejected.
- `GOLDEN-003`: a broken canonical action identity across the works seam,
  with no action-time decision, is rejected.
- Exact-head SHA pins are generated by skeleton runs, never hand-authored.

## Out of scope

Governance registers branches, vectors, and the pins shape only. Governance implements nothing in `Aftergraph/works-execution`; the canonical runner
belongs to the execution owners, proven by the owner repo's own tests, PR,
CI, merge queue, and exact-head evidence.
